What you will learn

Each session focuses on one part of building a fair and healthy workplace.

💰

Fair pay

How to understand wages, overtime, and benefits, and how to check that you are paid what you are owed.

🛡️

Safe conditions

Recognize workplace hazards early and learn how to raise concerns without fear of retaliation.

🤝

Respectful workplaces

Practical ways to handle discrimination, harassment, and unfair treatment on the job.

📜

Know your rights

A clear overview of contracts, working hours, leave, and where to get help when something goes wrong.

How it works

Three simple steps from signing up to applying what you learned.

1

Sign up

Reserve a seat in the session that suits you. Spots are limited so each person gets attention.

2

Attend the workshop

Join the interactive session, work through real examples, and share your own experiences.

3

Take the toolkit home

Leave with a checklist, contact list, and action plan you can use at work the same week.
